## 2. Context & Problems to Solve

When an operator runs `download-arsenal.sh` (generated by `OBOL.arsenal.buildScript()` in `assets/engine/arsenal.js` from `OBOL.ARSENAL` in `data/tools-install.js`) on a clean `kali-linux-default` installation, several gaps currently prevent commands from working out of the box:

1. **`obol_fetch` saves `.zip` URLs directly as `.exe` without unzipping**:
   - `procdump` uses `url: 'https://download.sysinternals.com/files/Procdump.zip'` and `dest: 'procdump64.exe'`. Because `obol_fetch` in `assets/engine/arsenal.js:72-76` has no archive extraction logic (unlike `obol_fetch_gh`), `curl` writes the raw `.zip` bytes straight into `procdump64.exe`.
2. **Linux staged tools and pivot agents are never copied to `$WWW` (`~/.obol/arsenal/www`)**:
   - `assets/engine/arsenal.js:140-141` only copies `*.exe`, `*.bat`, `*.ps1`, `*.zip`, and `*.msi` into `$WWW`.
   - `linpeas.sh`, `LinEnum.sh`, `linux-exploit-suggester.sh`, `pspy64`, `socat`, `chisel`, and `agent` are never copied into `$WWW` (or `$CACHE/lin`), causing 404s when serving `$WWW` to a Linux target.
3. **`chisel` and `ligolo-proxy` are not `chmod +x` or placed on `$PATH`, and target-side pivot agents are missing**:
   - `chisel` and `ligolo-ng` are `class: 'material'`, so they land in `~/.obol/arsenal/` without `chmod +x` and are not linked into `~/.local/bin`. Both `pivotkit.js` (`command -v ligolo-proxy`) and the paste-back inventory (`shutil.which`) report them missing.
   - `pivotkit.js` also instructs the operator to download `agent` (Linux), `agent.exe` (Windows), `chisel` (Linux), and `chisel.exe` (Windows) onto the target from their HTTP server, but `agent`, `agent.exe`, and `chisel.exe` are not fetched.
4. **`git` cloned tools in `~/tools/<repo>` are not on `$PATH` (and `sccmhunter` loses `&& pipx install`)**:
   - Moves invoke `windapsearch.py`, `targetedKerberoast.py`, `sccmhunter.py`, `pygpoabuse.py`, `PetitPotam.py`, `printerbug.py`, `dfscoerce.py`, `noPac.py`, `CVE-2021-1675.py`, `krbrelayx.py`, `dnstool.py`, `timeroast.py`, `zerologon-scan`, `set_empty_pw.py`, `reinstall_original_pw.py`, and `eternalblue_exploit7.py` as bare commands. Because `obol_git` only clones into `~/tools/<key>` without placing wrapper shims in `~/.local/bin`, running those commands from a workspace directory fails with `command not found`.
5. **Missing Staged Windows/Linux Payloads & Filename Aliases**:
   - `PrintSpoofer64.exe`, `JuicyPotato.exe`, `JuicyPotatoNG.exe`, `AccessChk.zip` (`accesschk64.exe`), `Certify.exe`, `Whisker.exe`, `KrbRelayUp.exe`, `Watson.exe`, `PowerUp.ps1`, `PrivescCheck.ps1`, `DomainPasswordSpray.ps1`, and `Invoke-PowerShellTcp.ps1` (`rev.ps1`) are referenced in moves and/or `data/tool-staging.js` (`OBOL.TOOL_STAGING`) but are missing from `data/tools-install.js`.
   - Moves and `tool-staging.js` use both `GodPotato-NET4.exe` and `GodPotato.exe`, `PrintSpoofer64.exe` and `PrintSpoofer.exe`, `accesschk64.exe` and `accesschk.exe`, `procdump64.exe` and `procdump.exe`, `nc64.exe` and `nc.exe`, `linux-exploit-suggester.sh` and `les.sh`, `Invoke-PowerShellTcp.ps1` and `rev.ps1`.
6. **Missing Kali Packages (`apt` / `pipx` / `git`) and `SecLists` on a Clean Kali Install**:
   - Clean `kali-linux-default` does not ship `/usr/share/seclists` (`seclists`), `bloodhound.py` (`bloodhound-python`), `feroxbuster`, `gobuster`, `redis-tools` (`redis-cli`), `finger`, `odat`, `mdbtools`, `python3-oletools` / `oletools` (`olevba`), `cisco-decrypt`, etc.

           local tmp; tmp=$(mktemp);

         fi;
         [ -s "$out" ] || { warn "$k: nothing extracted"; return; };
